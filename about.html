Who is Vũ Xuân Toàn?

Vũ Xuân Toàn (also written without diacritics as Vu Xuan Toan) is a Vietnamese software developer and indie maker. He was born on 4 December 2005 in Biển Động, Bắc Ninh, Vietnam. He is the founder of Vu Xuan Toan Tech, an independent software studio building AI-powered products for warehouse logistics, education and cloud automation.

What he builds

His public work includes Mầm Văn, a literature-learning platform for Vietnamese middle-school students with teacher grading tools and an AI content studio; the Vinh Giang warehouse management system (WMS), which combines real-time warehouse data with large-language-model APIs; Oracle Cloud infrastructure automation; and Facebook Messenger chatbots with Google Sheets integration.
