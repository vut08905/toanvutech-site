About Vũ Xuân Toàn (Vu Xuan Toan)

Vũ Xuân Toàn (also written Vu Xuan Toan) is a Vietnamese software developer and indie maker, born on 4 December 2005 in Biển Động, Bắc Ninh, Vietnam. He is the founder of Vu Xuan Toan Tech, where he designs and builds AI-powered software — from intelligent warehouse management systems to education platforms and cloud automation tools.

His public work includes the Mầm Văn education platform for Vietnamese literature students, the Vinh Giang warehouse management system (WMS), Oracle Cloud infrastructure automation, and Messenger chatbot integrations.
